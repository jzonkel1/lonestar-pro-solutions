Privacy Policy

Lonestar Pro Solutions (“we,” “us,” “our”) is a construction company based in Robstown, Texas. This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and what we do with it.

Information you give us

If you fill out the estimate form or call or text us, we collect what you choose to send: your name, phone number, email address if you provide one, the type of work you need, and whatever you tell us about the job or property. We only ask for what we need to give you an estimate.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our web host records standard server information when you visit — your IP address, browser type, the pages you viewed and the time of the visit. We use this only to keep the site working and secure.

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics on this site, and we do not set our own cookies.

Third-party services on this site

  • Google Fonts — type is loaded from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google Maps — our service-area map is embedded from Google and loads only when you scroll to it. Google may set its own cookies. Google's practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Our web host and form provider — they process page requests and deliver form submissions to us by email.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about the estimate or work you asked about
  • To schedule, quote and carry out the work
  • To answer questions and keep records of jobs we have done

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone except a service provider helping us run the business or where the law requires it.

Calls and text messages

If you call or text Lonestar Pro Solutions, your phone carrier handles that message under its own terms. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we will stop.

How long we keep it

We keep estimate requests and job records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our business and tax obligations, then we dispose of them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call or text (361) 742-1170 and we will take care of it.

Children

This site is meant for adults hiring a contractor. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
