Common Questions

Straight Answers

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Robstown and work across the Coastal Bend — Corpus Christi, Kingsville, Robstown, Portland and the surrounding communities.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free on every job. Call or text (361) 742-1170 and we'll come look at the work and give you a straight number.

What does your 100% workmanship guarantee cover?

It covers all of our own workmanship for 60 days after a job is finished. If something we installed fails in that window, we come back, assess it and fix it at no charge. It doesn't cover manufacturer defects in materials, weather damage, normal wear, or work done by someone else.

Do you handle commercial work, or just homes?

Both. The same crew that frames Coastal Bend homes also builds out businesses — ground-up commercial framing, exterior facades, waterproofing and storefront finish-outs.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We work with several lenders and can walk you through the options that fit the job.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Saturdays by appointment. Se habla español.
